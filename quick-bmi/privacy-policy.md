# Privacy Policy — Quick BMI Calculator

**Effective date:** October 21, 2026

Quick BMI Calculator is developed and published by FutureDevSpace.

## 1. Information We Collect

Quick BMI Calculator does not require an account or personal information to use its BMI calculation features.

Height and weight values entered into the app are processed locally on your device to calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI). The app does not transmit these values to our servers.

## 2. How Information Is Used

The information you enter is used to calculate your BMI and display the corresponding BMI category. BMI is a screening measure and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share the height, weight, or BMI values entered into the app with third parties.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

BMI calculations are performed on your device. We do not operate a server that stores your BMI calculations.

## 5. Third-Party Services

If third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting services, or other data-collecting components are added in the future, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Quick BMI Calculator is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not designed for children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
